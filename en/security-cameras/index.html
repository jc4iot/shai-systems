Start with coverage, not a camera count

Camera placement matters as much as the camera model. We review entrances, paths, parking, outdoor spaces or work areas, together with sunlight, night lighting and the available cable routes. The goal is useful footage without filling the property with unnecessary cameras.

For an existing system, we can first check the cabling, recorder and cameras to understand what may be retained and what is limiting image quality or reliability.

What we check before recommending a system

  • The distance and detail that each camera needs to capture.
  • Day and night lighting, glare and dark areas.
  • Cable routes, network connections, power and backup requirements.
  • Recording retention and user access permissions.
  • Possible future expansion or integration with other security systems.

Security camera questions

How many cameras does a home or business need?

The number depends on entrances, the areas that matter, desired overlap and the property layout. A site review helps avoid blind spots and unnecessary equipment.

Can I view the cameras on my phone?

Compatible systems can provide remote viewing and alerts. A reliable network connection, suitable equipment and secure user permissions are required.

Can existing cables or cameras be reused?

Sometimes. We first check the cable type and condition, recorder compatibility and required image quality. Not every mix of old and new equipment is stable.
