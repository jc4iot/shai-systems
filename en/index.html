What happens before installation

1. You explain the problem

Tell us what you want to see, control or secure and who will use the system.

2. We check the property

We look at cable routes, network coverage, existing equipment and the practical installation conditions.

3. We narrow down the options

The equipment and scope are chosen around compatibility, daily use and possible future additions.

4. The system is set up for use

After installation, the system is configured for the people who will actually use it.
