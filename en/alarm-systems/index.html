Protection that works with the routine

An alarm is more than a control panel and a motion detector. The design needs to consider entrances, windows, pets, working hours and the way people move through the property.

We also check where the control panel should sit, how different users will arm and disarm the system, what happens during a power interruption and which alerts are genuinely useful.

Alerts and camera integration

What we check before choosing a system

  • The number of entrances and areas that need separate arming.
  • Pets and their effect on detector type and placement.
  • Power and communication backup during a fault.
  • Simple operation for different users and required permissions.
  • Future compatibility with cameras, intercom or smart home systems.

Alarm system questions

Is a wired or wireless alarm better?

There is no single answer. Wired systems often suit new projects or properties with good existing cabling. Wireless systems may suit occupied properties. Coverage, reliability and maintenance all matter.

Can I arm only part of the property?

Compatible systems can be divided into zones and support partial arming. The zoning should follow the way people move through the building.

Can an existing alarm be upgraded?

Sometimes the cabling or some components can be retained. The control panel, detectors, wiring condition and expansion options need to be checked first.

Need to review an alarm system?

Tell us about the entrances, existing infrastructure and what should happen during an alert. That gives us a practical starting point.
